TCF Canada Exam Schedule – 2026
Alliance Française de Delhi is pleased to announce the
TCF Canada Exam Schedule for 2026
. The Test de Connaissance du Français (TCF) Canada is an official French language proficiency test required for
Canadian immigration and citizenship purposes
and is recognized by
Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC)
.
Alliance Française de Delhi conducts only TCF Canada examinations (to emigrate to Canada).
For other exams such as TCF TP, TEF Canada, TEF Québec, TCF ANF, TCF Québec, etc., please
click the link
to find information about other Alliance Française centres in India conducting them and their examination dates.
Note:
Limited Seats:
TCF Canada exam seats are limited and allocated on a first-come, first-served basis. Due to high demand, seats often fill within minutes of registration opening, for both online and offline registration modes. Candidates registering online are strongly advised to create their
registration account
in advance, so they can complete the booking and payment process quickly once slots become available. For offline registration, please arrive at the AFD Reception just before the registration opening time.
Waiting List:
Candidates who successfully secure a seat will receive a registration confirmation email. If all seats are filled, your registration will be placed on a waiting list. In this case, please contact the Administration/Front Desk for assistance — they will help transfer your registration fee to the same exam date (subject to availability of additional seats) or to the next available TCF exam date.

📝 Important Information for Candidates
The TCF Canada exam will be in a digital version and will be taken on a computer.
Registration details and deadlines will be shared separately for each session.
Candidates must carry a
valid passport (original)
on the exam day.
Reporting time and exam-day instructions will be communicated via email.
Late arrivals and electronic devices are
strictly prohibited
during the exam.
📩 Contact Information
For registration queries or further details, please contact AFD Frontdesk
Candidates are advised to regularly check the website and official communications for updates related to registration and exam procedures.
